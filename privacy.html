Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

bayan-post is an internal tool used only by the Bayan Motion channel team to publish the channel's own videos to the channel's own accounts.

What the tool accesses

  • Access tokens that the account owner grants through each platform's official sign-in (Google, Meta, TikTok), limited to uploading and publishing videos and reading the basic profile of the account being posted to.
  • The channel's own video files, cover images and descriptions, stored on the team's computers.

What the tool does not do

  • It does not collect, store or share personal data of viewers or of any other user.
  • It does not read messages, comments, followers or analytics.
  • It does not sell or transfer any data to third parties, and has no advertising or tracking.

Storage and security

Access tokens are kept only on the team's own computers, outside any shared or public storage, and are used only to send the channel's videos to the platform that issued them. They can be revoked at any time from the account's security settings on each platform (for Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Use of Google user data

bayan-post's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service (youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a message to @bayan_motion on Instagram.
