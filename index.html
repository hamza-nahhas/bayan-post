bayan-post

bayan-post is a private, internal publishing tool of the Bayan Motion channel (@bayan_motion), which makes short animated films explaining verses of the Quran, hadith and sayings of the early Muslims.

The tool runs only on the channel team's own computers. It uploads the channel's own finished films, with their cover image and description, to the channel's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok through each platform's official API. It is not offered to the public and does not act on anyone else's account.
