Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 4 November 2026

bayan-post does not collect or store personal data of any user. The only data it keeps is the access token that the Bayan Motion channel owner grants for the channel's own Facebook Page, Instagram account, YouTube channel and TikTok account, stored on the owner's own computer.

To remove bayan-post's access and data

  1. Facebook / Instagram: go to Facebook Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites), select Bayan manager, and click Remove. The token stops working immediately.
  2. YouTube / Google: go to myaccount.google.com/permissions, select bayan-post, and remove access.
  3. TikTok: in the TikTok app go to Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions, select bayan-post, and remove it.

The channel team deletes the locally stored tokens at the same time. Questions: send a message to @bayan_motion on Instagram.
